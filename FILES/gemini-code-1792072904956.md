# Executive Product Vision & Strategy Report: The Pre-Legal Technical Scoping Engine
**Target Audience:** Technical Founders, Engineering & Product Leads  
**Context:** October 2026 European Regulatory Landscape  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Thesis
Building an automated European Union "compliance brain" as a horizontal, all-company-size engine selling legal verdicts across eight frameworks with an in-house reviewer is both commercially and legally non-viable. However, pivoting the product into a **Pre-Legal Technical Scoping and Architecture-to-Law Translation Engine** for the **EU Digital & Cyber Quartet (AI Act, DORA, NIS2, CRA)** is a high-value, defensible opportunity. 

Instead of acting as an "automated outside counsel," the product automates the painful technical discovery that consumes 80% of outside legal billable hours—mapping GitHub repositories, cloud configurations, Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers, API gateways, and vendor sub-processors directly to statutory triggers. This hands a verified factual dossier to external law firms or in-house counsel for final sign-off, turning law firms into a high-margin distribution channel rather than legal adversaries.

## 2. Regulatory Reality & Market Urgency (October 2026)
* **The EU AI Act:** Prohibited practices and Article 50 transparency are fully enforceable. Standalone High-Risk obligations (Annex III) were postponed by the 2026 Digital Omnibus to **December 2, 2027**, but scoping demand is urgent: 40% of enterprise AI systems sit in an "unclear grey zone" where classification requires granular technical proof under Article 6(3)/6(4).
* **DORA:** Fully applicable since January 2025; 2026 brings active National Competent Authority (NCA) audits. In the ESA dry run, **93.5% of financial entities failed** their Register of Information submissions (86% due to missing technical sub-processor chains and LEIs).
* **Cyber Resilience Act (CRA):** Article 14 mandatory **24-hour reporting of actively exploited vulnerabilities** went live on **September 11, 2026**.
* **CSRD & NIS2:** The February 2026 Omnibus I Directive eliminated mandatory CSRD reporting for companies under 1,000 employees. NIS2 compliance remains fragmented across 27 transposing national laws.

---

## 3. Legal & Structural Risk Mitigation
* **The *Smartlaw* Safe Harbor (§ 2 Abs. 1 RDG):** To avoid Unauthorized Practice of Law (UPL) violations in Germany and France, the product splits into two layers:
  * **Layer 1:** Partner law firms author deterministic Yes/No threshold decision trees (headcount, NACE codes) executing purely mechanical logic (*Smartlaw* safe harbor).
  * **Layer 2:** An AI agent compiles engineering evidence. Grey-law judgments (*Einzelfallprüfung*) are never autogenerated as final verdicts; they are routed directly to external partner law firms under a separate attorney-client mandate.
* **Liability & EU Product Liability Directive (2024/2853):** From December 9, 2026, standalone software and SaaS face strict no-fault liability. Framing the product as an *evidence compiler* rather than a legal advisory service insulates the startup's balance sheet.

## 5. Strategic Product Roadmap & Feature Wedges

1. **Wedge 1 (AI Act):** Shadow AI/agent discovery + Art. 50 tracking + Art. 6(4) non-high-risk exemption dossier generation ahead of Dec 2027.
2. **Wedge 2 (DORA):** Automated ICT sub-contractor mapping (Template `B_05`), GLEIF LEI validation, and 15-table `xBRL-CSV` Register of Information compilation.
3. **Wedge 3 (CRA & NIS2):** Unified Multi-Regime Incident & Vulnerability Triage linking CI/CD SBOMs and CVE feeds directly to 24-hour vulnerability reporting.
4. **Go-To-Market & Pricing:** Target European upper-mid-market tech providers and regulated deployers (**100 to 2,000 employees**) at **€9,000 to €18,000/year** with unlimited internal seats, distributing through partner law firms.
