# Regulift

**Regulation, lifted. Compliance platform. Inside-out assessment.**

Regulift assesses compliance from inside your own systems and prepares it for real lawyers, so companies spend less on legal work and more on competing. We are building this for small and medium-sized European companies to eliminate the billable hours spent on fact-finding.

## 🌟 The Regulift Way: Inside-Out
Instead of relying on forms, questionnaires, and interviews that are out of date the following week, Regulift takes an **inside-out** approach. 
* Connects to your repositories, cloud, HR, and CRM tools.
* Builds your data and AI inventory from real workflows rather than recollection.
* Stays current automatically as your systems change.
* Hands partner lawyers organized evidence, so their hours go to judgment.

## ⚖️ Four Frameworks in One View
The goal is one platform for every regulation your company has to answer to. Our starting set includes:
* **GDPR** (Data protection)
* **EU AI Act** (Artificial intelligence)
* **NIS2** (Cybersecurity)
* **DORA** (Operational resilience)

## ⚙️ How It Works
1. **Understand:** AI does the groundwork by mapping your data flows and building the context for an assessment that fits your business.
2. **Improve:** See where evidence is missing and what to do about it. Each gap becomes a concrete action, ranked by what matters most. Most gaps can be closed without a lawyer.
3. **Review:** Partner lawyers are ready inside Regulift when you are. They open the exact same workspace—with your inventory, evidence, and open items already in place—and evaluate your formal outcome.

## 🔒 Built on Trust
* **Read-only by design:** We never change your systems. We only read what the assessment needs, and you can revoke access at any time.
* **EU Hosted:** Your materials and findings stay exclusively on European infrastructure, under European law.
* **No lock-in:** You own the record. Export your inventory and evidence whenever you want.
