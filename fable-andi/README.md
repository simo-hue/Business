# fable-andi

Working folder for the strategy, research and pitch material produced with Andi in October 2026.
Paths inside the handoffs are relative to this folder.

- `HANDOFF-strategy.md`: entry point for the strategy session (state, decisions, legal map, agenda).
- `HANDOFF-pitch-deck.md`: how the decks were built and how to rebuild them.
- `pitch/`: the three decks and their generators (`pitch/src/`).
- `FILES/`: the research reports with graded sources (regulations, countries, commercial, legal verification of the open markets).

The website itself lives in `regulift/`. `FILES/number-claims-sources.md` and `HANDOFF-website-content.md` at the repo root predate this folder.
