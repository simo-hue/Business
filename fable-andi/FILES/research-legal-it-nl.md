# Legal-services rules in Italy and the Netherlands: re-verification for Regulift

Date: 18 October 2026. Scope: who may give legal advice, how Regulift may be paid by partner law firms, whether Regulift may hold a stake in a firm, and the AI rules for lawyers. Grades: A primary statute or court text, B official secondary or specialist law publisher, C reputable press or survey, D snippet, U unverified. This is research, not legal advice. Have a local lawyer in each country review the final structure.

## Verdicts

**Italy: partly open, medium confidence.** The statute is still narrow. Law 247/2012, art. 2(6), reserves out-of-court legal advice to lawyers only where it is "connessa all'attività giurisdizionale" and done "in modo continuativo, sistematico e organizzato" (A). The new reform law, Law 137/2026, in force since 16 August 2026, keeps the same wording (A). So GDPR and NIS2 readiness work that is not tied to a dispute is not reserved. But the criminal courts read "connected" broadly: advice "destinata a incidere su un contenzioso giudiziale, presente o futuro" is enough, with no court mandate needed (Cass. pen. VI n. 18734/2025, B). Unauthorised practice is a crime under art. 348 of the penal code, with six months to three years in prison (A). The reform also tells the government to make void any payment to non-lawyers for litigation-connected legal advice (A, not yet in force). Lawyers may not pay anyone a commission for a client or a mandate (CDF art. 37(2), A). An outside investor may hold up to one third of a lawyers' company (A). Calling Italy "open" was too strong. It is open for readiness work that stays away from sanctions, incidents and disputes, and closed for referral commissions.

**Netherlands: open, high confidence on advice, medium confidence on fees.** No law reserves out-of-court legal advice to lawyers. The Advocatenwet protects only the title (art. 9a, A), and the only monopoly is court representation outside the cantonal court (Rv art. 79(2), A). The conduct rules allow a lawyer to pay or receive a fee for obtaining or referring mandates, but only if the lawyer can show the core values are respected and only the client's interest decides (Gedragsregel 2(3), A). The official explanation prefers a fixed amount or a fixed subscription over a percentage, and requires transparency to the client (A). Sharing "rekening en risico" with a non-lawyer is prohibited, even for "een heel klein deel" (Voda arts. 5.3 and 5.4, A). Shares in a law firm may be held only by lawyers and other admitted professions (Voda art. 5.8, A). The NOvA announced on 29 September 2026 that it wants to allow all firm structures in future, but this is a policy plan, not law (C).

## Italy

### 1. Reserved activities under Law 247/2012

**Current text, art. 2(5) and 2(6).** Normattiva shows the text in force on 18 October 2026. The law was last updated on 11 August 2026, but arts. 2 and 4-bis read as below (https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:legge:2012-12-31;247~art2, A).

> "5. Sono attività esclusive dell'avvocato, fatti salvi i casi espressamente previsti dalla legge, l'assistenza, la rappresentanza e la difesa nei giudizi davanti a tutti gli organi giurisdizionali e nelle procedure arbitrali rituali."

> "6. Fuori dei casi in cui ricorrono competenze espressamente individuate relative a specifici settori del diritto e che sono previste dalla legge per gli esercenti altre professioni regolamentate, l'attività professionale di consulenza legale e di assistenza legale stragiudiziale, ove connessa all'attività giurisdizionale, se svolta in modo continuativo, sistematico e organizzato, è di competenza degli avvocati."

Art. 2(6) then allows in-house legal advice to an employer and its group companies. Art. 2(7) reserves the title "avvocato" to people who are or were on a bar roll.

**The reform keeps the same test.** Law 137 of 28 July 2026 delegates a full reform of the legal profession to the government. It was published in GU n. 177 of 1 August 2026 and has been in force since 16 August 2026 (https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:legge:2026-07-28;137, A). Art. 2(1)(a)(3.2) repeats the art. 2(6) wording word for word. It widens the court reservation in point 3.1 to "negoziazione assistita" and mandatory or court-ordered mediation. Point 4 adds a new rule:

> "disponga la nullità di ogni pattuizione avente a oggetto il pagamento di corrispettivi, in qualunque forma, in favore di soggetti non iscritti all'albo degli avvocati quale compenso per attività di consulenza legale e assistenza legale, ove connesse all'attività giurisdizionale"

(https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:legge:2026-07-28;137~art2, A). The Council of Ministers approved the draft decree in preliminary reading on 2 October 2026. Reports say the draft keeps the reservation and the nullity rule (misterlex.it, 5 Oct 2026, C). The draft text itself was not found. The government has six months from 16 August 2026 to adopt it.

**Penalty.** Art. 348 of the penal code punishes "Chiunque abusivamente esercita una professione per la quale è richiesta una speciale abilitazione dello Stato" with "la reclusione da sei mesi a tre anni e con la multa da euro 10.000 a euro 50.000". A professional who "ha determinato altri" or directed the activity faces one to five years (Brocardi copy of the code, B; Normattiva did not render the single article). For a partner lawyer, CDF art. 36(2) adds a disciplinary offence for helping non-lawyers practise or "ne possano ricavare benefici economici", punished by two to six months' suspension (A, see section 3).

### 2. Case law on non-lawyer consultants

**Cass. pen. sez. VI n. 18734 of 19 May 2025.** The defendant, not a lawyer, advised a company for about eight months for over €7,000. He was hired to file an opposition to a payment order, which he never filed. The court upheld the conviction. Edotto, a specialist publisher, reports the reasoning: the activity was "organizzata, sistematica, continuativa e finalizzata a gestire contenziosi giudiziali in atto o potenziali", and the advice "era destinata a incidere su un contenzioso giudiziale, presente o futuro". The court cited Cass. 34713/2023, which says the connected work may be "anche solo quella preparatoria", and Cass. 21565/2020 on settlements (https://edotto.com/articolo/assistenza-stragiudiziale-quando-si-configura-lesercizio-abusivo-della-professione, B). The full text is on italgiure, which did not respond from here. The edotto PDF needs a login. So the grade rises from D to B, not A.

**Cass. pen. SS.UU. n. 11545 of 23 March 2012.** This is the "atti caratteristici" test. The official maxim says art. 348 covers acts that, "pur non attribuiti singolarmente in via esclusiva a una determinata professione, siano univocamente individuati come di competenza specifica di essa", when done "per continuatività, onerosità e organizzazione" so as to create "le oggettive apparenze di un'attività professionale svolta da soggetto regolarmente abilitato". The case concerned accountants, and the court found that bookkeeping and tax returns by non-members did not meet the test (https://brocardi.it/massimario/35068.html, B).

**Cass. pen. sez. VI n. 47675 of 30 October 2023.** The maxim says any act "idoneo ad incidere sulla progressione del procedimento" on someone's behalf is reserved, "mentre esulano dagli atti tipici della professione le attività di consulenza legale, che possono divenire rilevanti solo se svolte in modo continuativo" (https://brocardi.it/massimario/81025.html, B).

**What this means for GDPR and NIS2 readiness.** No decision, CNF opinion or AGCM act was found that applies art. 348 to a GDPR, NIS2 or legal-tech provider. The following is our reading, not a court finding.

- Readiness work is about future compliance, not a dispute. The statute needs a connection to "attività giurisdizionale". GDPR and NIS2 sanctions come from administrative authorities, the Garante and ACN. A court is involved only if the company later opposes a sanction.
- The 2025 ruling stretches "connected" to future disputes. But its facts involved a payment order already served. A pure readiness check is several steps further away.
- The 2012 test asks whether acts are "univocamente" lawyers' work. GDPR and NIS2 gap analysis is done in Italy by engineers, auditors and consultants, so it is hard to call it uniquely lawyers' work.
- The risk rises sharply when Regulift's output turns to defence. Examples are advice on how to answer a Garante or ACN inspection, on whether an incident creates liability to customers, on contesting a fine, or on a supplier dispute. These are "preparatory" to possible proceedings in the sense of Cass. 34713/2023.
- Product consequence: in Italy, the platform may state applicability and gaps for readiness. Anything about sanctions, inspections, incidents with third-party claims, or disputes should go to the partner lawyer.

### 3. Code of conduct (CDF), current text

Source: the CNF's consolidated code, "agg. 2026" (https://www.consiglionazionaleforense.it/documents/20182/40227/NUOVO+CODICE+DEONTOLOGICO+FORENSE++-+agg.+2026.pdf, A). The site codicedeontologico-cnf.it blocked access from this region.

**Art. 37, divieto di accaparramento di clientela.**

> "1. L'avvocato non deve acquisire rapporti di clientela a mezzo di agenzie o procacciatori o con modi non conformi a correttezza e decoro.
> 2. L'avvocato non deve offrire o corrispondere a colleghi o a terzi provvigioni o altri compensi quale corrispettivo per la presentazione di un cliente o per l'ottenimento di incarichi professionali.
> 3. Costituisce infrazione disciplinare l'offerta di omaggi o prestazioni a terzi ovvero la corresponsione o la promessa di vantaggi per ottenere difese o incarichi."

Comma 5 bans offering "una prestazione personalizzata" to a specific person for a specific matter without being asked. The sanction is censure.

**Art. 35, dovere di corretta informazione.** Information "quali che siano i mezzi utilizzati" must respect "verità, correttezza, trasparenza". The lawyer must not give information that is "comparative con altri professionisti né equivoche, ingannevoli, denigratorie, suggestive". Comma 6 bans naming professionals or third parties "non organicamente o direttamente collegati con lo studio". The sanction is censure. Art. 17 repeats that public information "con qualunque mezzo, anche informatico" must be "non comparative".

**Art. 36(2).** It is a disciplinary offence for a lawyer to make it possible for non-lawyers to practise unlawfully "o consenta che tali soggetti ne possano ricavare benefici economici".

**What this means for fees.**

- A fee per mandate or per introduced client paid by the firm to Regulift falls squarely under art. 37(2). Not allowed.
- A percentage of the firm's fees is a "provvigione". Not allowed.
- A fixed subscription for software, workspace seats and services is not a payment "per la presentazione di un cliente". No source says it is banned. It must not rise with the number of mandates.
- Platforms are not banned as such. The CNF's 2012 opinion n. 48 against the discount platform Amica Card led the AGCM to open case I748 for restricting competition (https://www.agcm.it/dotcmsDOC/allegati-news/I748_ch._istr._omi.pdf, A for the AGCM findings). Secondary sources report a fine of about €912,536 in 2014, confirmed by the Consiglio di Stato in judgment 1164/2016 (studiocataldi.it, C). One blog cites a CNF ruling n. 186 of 4 July 2025 on lawyers taking mandates through an agency. It was not found (D).
- Comparative ranking of lawyers inside the platform could trouble the partner firm under arts. 17 and 35, because a lawyer may not give comparative information. This is our reading (U). Matching by expertise without "best lawyer" claims is safer.

### 4. Lawyers' companies, art. 4-bis

Current text (https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:legge:2012-12-31;247~art4bis, A):

> "a) i soci, per almeno due terzi del capitale sociale e dei diritti di voto, devono essere avvocati iscritti all'albo, ovvero avvocati iscritti all'albo e professionisti iscritti in albi di altre professioni"

A majority of the board must be lawyer-partners. Board members must be partners. The mandate stays personal to the lawyer. Holding through trusts or nominees is banned.

**Change coming under Law 137/2026, art. 2(1)(h)(9) to (11), A.** The two-thirds rule stays. New limits for the non-professional partner:

- Non-professional partners are allowed "soltanto per prestazioni tecniche o per finalità di investimento".
- Their profit share must be proportional. For work done for the non-professional partner's own group it follows the profits generated. For other work it may be higher, but "non superiore al 33 per cento di quella spettante ai soci professionisti".
- The company "non possa prestare attività in misura prevalente a favore del socio non professionista o di soggetti da esso controllati o collegati".

So Regulift could still invest up to one third in an Italian lawyers' company once the decree passes. The firm's clients would be Regulift's customers, not Regulift's group, so the "prevalent activity" limit should not bite. That last point is our reading (U). Combined with art. 37, the stake must not become a channel for paying for referrals.

### 5. AI rules: Law 132/2025 and its decrees

**Art. 13 of Law 132/2025, current text** (https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:legge:2025-09-23;132~art13, A):

> "1. L'utilizzo di sistemi di intelligenza artificiale nelle professioni intellettuali è finalizzato al solo esercizio delle attività strumentali e di supporto all'attività professionale e con prevalenza del lavoro intellettuale oggetto della prestazione d'opera.
> 2. Per assicurare il rapporto fiduciario tra professionista e cliente, le informazioni relative ai sistemi di intelligenza artificiale utilizzati dal professionista sono comunicate al soggetto destinatario della prestazione intellettuale con linguaggio chiaro, semplice ed esaustivo."

**Who it binds.** The duty falls on "il professionista" toward "il soggetto destinatario della prestazione intellettuale". That is the partner law firm toward its client. A software platform that licenses a tool is not performing a "prestazione d'opera intellettuale", so art. 13 does not bind Regulift as such. If Regulift sells human compliance consulting, art. 13 could apply to that service, because it covers all "professioni intellettuali", not only regulated ones. This is our reading (U). In practice, Regulift should give partner firms a ready-made description of its AI systems so they can meet the duty.

**CNF guidance.** On 13 October 2025 the CNF issued a model client notice on AI use. It is a transparency statement the client signs. It lists AI uses as "strumentali e/o di supporto", such as research, document analysis and drafts, and promises human verification (ildiritto.it, 15 Oct 2025, B). In April 2026 CNF president Greco said the CNF is mapping the legal-tech market, has launched a European tender for AI tools on a voluntary "convenzione" model, and will turn the Milan Bar's "Carta di Milano" and other bar documents into national guidelines (https://www.consiglionazionaleforense.it/it/web/cnf-news/-/24697-408, 13 Apr 2026, A). No national CNF AI guidelines were found as of today.

A search summary linked the CNF's February 2026 change, made after AGCM objections, to the AI notice. That is wrong. The change concerned art. 25-bis on fair fees (equo compenso), now limited to large clients (ildiritto.it, 15 Apr 2026, B).

**Legislative Decree 179 of 7 October 2026.** It was published in GU n. 234 of 8 October 2026 and enters into force on 23 October 2026 (https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:decreto.legislativo:2026-10-07;179, A).

- Art. 47 says continuing-education courses run by the professional orders "includono percorsi di alfabetizzazione e formazione all'uso dei sistemi di intelligenza artificiale", with a minimum number of hours. The content includes "obblighi informativi verso il cliente". The CNF must adapt its rules within six months.
- Art. 49 says AI use "può comportare una modulazione dell'equo compenso" according to the AI Act risk class of the system. Ministerial fee parameters must be updated within twelve months.
- Nothing in the decree regulates legal-tech providers or reserves AI legal assessments.

A first decree, D.Lgs. 160/2026 on AI liability, was reported but not opened (C).

### 6. Other 2025 to 2026 developments

- The Garante's 2026 annual report focused on AI. A press report says its inspection plan for the second half of 2026 extends checks to DPO providers and AI systems (agendadigitale.eu, C). No Garante decision or guideline specific to compliance platforms reading company systems was found (U). Regulift needs a normal processor agreement and a DPIA where personal data is involved.
- The AGCM has pushed back on the CNF twice: Amica Card in 2014, and the fair-fee rule in 2026. This suggests a total ban on lawyers using platforms would not survive competition law. Commissions are a separate matter, because art. 37(2) targets the payment itself.

## Netherlands

### 1. No monopoly on legal advice

**Advocatenwet art. 9a**, in force since 1 January 2026 (https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002093/2026-01-01, A):

> "Tot het voeren van de titel van advocaat is uitsluitend gerechtigd degene die als advocaat is ingeschreven op grond van artikel 1, eerste lid, of 2a, eerste lid."

**Court representation, Rv art. 79** (wetten.overheid.nl, BWBR0001827, A):

> "1 Partijen kunnen in zaken voor de kantonrechter in persoon procederen.
> 2 In alle overige zaken kunnen partijen niet in persoon procederen, maar slechts bij advocaat."

No provision in the Advocatenwet reserves out-of-court advice. The NOvA's own explanation of Gedragsregel 2(3) names the "procesmonopolie" as the lawyer's special position, not an advice monopoly (A). The Wet op de rechtsbijstand regulates subsidised legal aid and was not found to create any reservation (A for the absence in the Advocatenwet, U for a full search of all Dutch statutes).

So in the Netherlands, Regulift may give company-specific legal assessments itself. The limits are general law: liability for wrong advice, misleading B2B advertising under BW art. 6:194, and not using the title "advocaat". The Dutch Bar has no power over non-lawyers.

### 2. Referral fees and fee sharing

**Gedragsregel 2(3), Gedragsregels advocatuur 2018** (https://www.advocatenorde.nl/voor-advocaten/wet-en-regelgeving/gedragsregels/gedragsregels-advocatuur/de-maatschappelijke-rol, A):

> "Het is de advocaat niet toegestaan een beloning toe te kennen of te ontvangen voor het verkrijgen of aanbrengen van opdrachten, tenzij de advocaat kan aantonen dat hij daarbij niet handelt in strijd met de kernwaarden en voorts dat hierbij slechts het belang van de rechtzoekende bepalend is."

The official explanation names "bemiddelingswebsites" and sets four conditions: the client's free choice of lawyer is not undermined, there is no entanglement of financial interests, third parties get no stake in the outcome, and the client can see what happens to the matter. It cites HvD 7 September 2018, ECLI:NL:TAHVD:2018:178. Its practical checklist:

- The lawyer must tell the client that the matter was referred, by whom, and what fee was paid.
- Expertise must be the primary matching criterion. Selection criteria must be "duidelijk en transparant". Matching should happen "per individuele zaak", not in batches.
- The client must remain free not to hire the lawyer. The referral is "een suggestie en niet als een dwingende voordracht".
- The fee must be reasonable for the value of the matter. "Een vast bedrag" influences independence less "dan een percentage van de (potentiele) omzet. Datzelfde geldt voor een vast abonnementstarief."
- "Uiteindelijk is echter het oordeel van de tuchtrechter bepalend."

The conduct rules are guidance, not a regulation. The disciplinary courts apply the core values in Advocatenwet art. 10a and the Voda. ECLI:NL:TADRARL:2022:2 says breaching a conduct rule is not automatically culpable (tuchtrecht.overheid.nl, A, read via search summary only, so D for its exact words).

**HvD 7 September 2018, ECLI:NL:TAHVD:2018:178.** Four lawyers used a claims foundation for Groningen gas-extraction victims. The court found "evidente verstrengeling van financiële belangen en een ongeoorloofde beloning of provisie" and upheld a reprimand (https://tuchtrecht.overheid.nl/ECLI_NL_TAHVD_2018_178, A).

No 2023 to 2026 disciplinary ruling on a platform charging per lead or per case was found (U). The injury-lawyers association LSA bans any fee per lead or case for its members, which is stricter than the general rule (letselschade.nu, C).

**Fee sharing with a non-lawyer is prohibited.** Voda art. 5.3 defines a partnership as practising "voor gezamenlijke rekening en risico" or sharing "de zeggenschap of eindverantwoordelijkheid". Art. 5.4 allows partnerships only with lawyers, foreign lawyers, notaries, tax advisers and patent attorneys. The explanation says sharing the proceeds of a matter is joint account and risk, "of het een heel klein deel is of meer substantieel". It also says pure referral of clients is not a partnership (https://www.advocatenorde.nl/voor-advocaten/wet-en-regelgeving/verordening-op-de-advocatuur, A).

**No cure no pay.** Voda art. 7.7 bans fees due only on success and fees as a share of the value obtained, except debt collection (art. 7.8) and injury cases under paragraph 7.4.3 (A). This limits the firm's pricing toward the client. It is not a rule about Regulift.

**Result.** The earlier "fee sharing conditional (U)" becomes two separate findings:

- A referral fee from the firm to Regulift is conditionally allowed (A). Prefer a fixed fee per referral or a flat subscription, disclosed to the client, with expertise-led matching and free choice.
- A share of the firm's fees or profits is not allowed (A).

### 3. Ownership

**Voda art. 5.8(1).** All shares and voting rights of a practice company must be held by lawyers or admitted professionals working in it, or by holding companies run only by them. **Art. 5.8(3)** lets non-lawyer staff of the firm together hold economic rights to "ten hoogste tien procent van de winst" (A). A non-employee company such as Regulift cannot hold shares.

**Reform, not law.** The independent Winter committee reported on 29 May 2026. It recommends phased opening under conditions, and says non-functional investors, "waaronder private equity", should not be allowed while the new supervisor OTA is still being built. It refers to the CJEU ruling that member states may ban purely financial investors (https://www.advocatenorde.nl/nieuws/aanbevelingen-commissie-vormen-opstap-naar-toekomstbestendige-regelgeving-voor-alternatieve, A for the NOvA statement). The CJEU case is C-295/23 Halmer, already in the earlier research (A). On 29 September 2026 the NOvA announced it wants to allow "in principe alle kantoorvormen". Lawyers would sit in a separate unit with "doorslaggevende zeggenschap", and external financiers may have no "feitelijke invloed". Timing depends on the OTA (advocatenblad.nl, 29 Sep 2026, C). The insurer experiment in Voda arts. 5.16 and 5.17 concerns lawyers employed by legal-expenses insurers, not general ABS (A).

### 4. Software and AI giving legal assessments

- No Dutch rule restricts software or AI from giving company-specific legal assessments (U, no provision found).
- The NOvA's "Aanbevelingen AI in de advocatuur" (late 2025) bind only lawyers. They say "De advocaat blijft eindverantwoordelijk", "Verifieer altijd de output", "Gebruik uitsluitend tools met bronvermelding", ask lawyers to check how vendors use data, to run a DPIA, and to "Vraag toestemming aan de cliënt voor AI-gebruik in het dossier" (https://www.advocatenorde.nl/voor-advocaten/digitalisering-ai/aanbevelingen-ai-de-advocatuur, A). Partner firms will ask Regulift for source citations, data-flow maps, data location and a DPIA. Build these in.
- Consumer law: Regulift sells B2B, so unfair commercial practices law for consumers does not apply. Misleading B2B advertising falls under BW art. 6:194. Not researched further (U).

### 5. NIS2 and AI Act status

- **NIS2.** The Cyberbeveiligingswet is Stb. 2026, 187, published 10 July 2026 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-187.html, A). The Cyberbeveiligingsbesluit, Stb. 2026, 189, sets its entry into force at 15 August 2026 (njb.nl, B). Confirmed.
- **AI Act supervision.** The earlier label "RDI pending" needs correcting. The draft Uitvoeringswet AI-verordening went to consultation on 20 April 2026 until 1 June 2026. Existing regulators supervise within their own sectors. For areas without a clear regulator, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens is proposed as supervisor with a special AI board member. The AP and the RDI coordinate (https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/ministeries/ministerie-van-economische-zaken-en-klimaat/nieuws/2026/04/20/kabinet-zet-stap-met-toezicht-op-europese-ai-regels, A). No bill passed or sent to parliament was found as of today (U).
