# Legal-services rules in Poland, Belgium and Switzerland: what Regulift may do

Prepared 2026-10-18 as a follow-up to research-countries.md, section 4.6. That file labelled the legal-advice rules of these three countries "open (U)". This file checks that label against primary sources. All sources were accessed on 2026-10-18.

Grades follow research-countries.md: **A** statute, gazette, court decision or official body; **B** official secondary, such as a bar's own decision or report; **C** reputable press or practitioner article; **D** search snippet or secondary blog; **U** not verified in this session.

This is research, not legal advice. Have a local lawyer in each country review the structure before launch.

## 1. Verdicts

**Poland: partly open (high confidence).** Out-of-court legal advice to businesses is not reserved to lawyers. No statute reserves it, and the only attempt to regulate non-lawyer advice by statute was struck down by the Constitutional Tribunal in 2006 (K 6/06 and K 30/06). So Regulift may tell a customer "NIS2 applies to you" without breaking a monopoly. The money side is closed, as in Austria. Both bar codes ban paying or receiving anything for referring a client, and the radca code bans sharing client fees with an outside acquirer. Only lawyers and a few listed professions may be partners in a law firm. Two 2026 changes matter: the titles "kancelaria adwokacka" and "kancelaria radcy prawnego" are now protected, and since 17 October 2026 it is a crime to compile and publish a register of advocates' list data without bar consent.

**Belgium: partly open (medium to high confidence).** Only pleading in court is reserved (Code judiciaire art. 440). The Brussels bar itself states that legal services outside the monopoly are "non protégée". So applicability statements and gap ratings by Regulift are lawful. I found no "2017 law on legal advice" that reserves "consultation juridique", and I believe it does not exist; this point stays open until a Belgian lawyer confirms it. The money side is closed for referral fees and fee shares. Both bar codes ban both. The current AVOCATS.BE code (in force 30 Apr 2026) expressly lets a lawyer pay an online intermediary "pour le service qu'il offre et les frais qu'il expose", which covers a flat workspace fee. Flemish lawyers may not form multidisciplinary associations with non-lawyers. On the French-speaking side only listed natural persons, such as former partners, relatives and administrative staff, may hold a minority of a firm's capital, so a company like Regulift may not.

**Switzerland: open for advice, closed for fee flows (high confidence).** Federal law regulates only court representation. The Federal Council told Parliament in 2018: "Die Rechtsberatungstätigkeit ist in der Schweiz nicht reguliert." Zurich reserves only representation in civil and criminal proceedings. Regulift could even employ lawyers to give advice itself, which is impossible in Austria. The SAV rules ban referral commissions and require platforms to respect that ban. Non-registered persons may not hold shares in a firm whose lawyers are on a cantonal register (BGE 144 II 147). Switzerland has no AI law. The consultation draft promised for end 2026 had not been published by 18 October 2026.

**What contradicts the earlier "open" label.** The label was right for advice in all three countries. It was wrong if read as "open" for the partner-firm economics. Per-mandate fees, fee shares and equity stakes are as restricted as in Austria in all three. The new Polish offence on lawyer registers was not known before.

## 2. Poland

### 2.1 Is paid out-of-court legal advice reserved to lawyers?

No. The two professional statutes describe what lawyers do, but they do not forbid others from doing it.

- **Prawo o adwokaturze, art. 4(1)**, consolidated text Dz.U. 2024 poz. 1564: "Zawód adwokata polega na świadczeniu pomocy prawnej, a w szczególności na udzielaniu porad prawnych, sporządzaniu opinii prawnych, opracowywaniu projektów aktów prawnych oraz występowaniu przed sądami i urzędami." Gloss: the advocate's profession consists of providing legal assistance, in particular legal advice, legal opinions, drafting legal acts and appearing before courts and offices. https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2024/1564/text.pdf (A)
- **Ustawa o radcach prawnych, art. 4 and art. 6(1)**, Dz.U. 2024 poz. 499: "Wykonywanie zawodu radcy prawnego polega na świadczeniu pomocy prawnej." Art. 6(1) lists "udzielaniu porad i konsultacji prawnych, sporządzaniu opinii prawnych". Gloss: the profession consists of legal assistance, including advice, consultations and opinions. https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2024/499/text.pdf (A)
- **What is protected is the title.** Prawo o adwokaturze art. 1(4): "Tytuł zawodowy „adwokat" podlega ochronie prawnej." Radcy act art. 2(2) says the same for "radca prawny". (A)
- **The Constitutional Tribunal rulings.** The 2005 amendment (Dz.U. 2005 nr 163 poz. 1361) added art. 4(1a) to the advocates' act: "Przepis ust. 1 nie stanowi przeszkody do świadczenia pomocy prawnej, o której mowa w ust. 1, przez osoby posiadające wyższe wykształcenie prawnicze, z wyjątkiem zastępstwa procesowego..." Gloss: paragraph 1 does not prevent persons with a law degree from providing legal assistance, except court representation. The Tribunal held this provision "niezgodny z art. 2 Konstytucji" (K 6/06, 19 Apr 2006, Dz.U. 2006 nr 75 poz. 529). It held the parallel radca provision, art. 6(2), unconstitutional in K 30/06 of 8 Nov 2006 (Dz.U. 2006 nr 206 poz. 1522). Both lost force on 31 Dec 2006, as footnotes in the consolidated texts confirm. https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2005/1361/text.pdf, https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2006/529/text.pdf, https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2006/1522/text.pdf (A for the operative parts. I could not open the full reasoning on trybunal.gov.pl.)
- **What this means.** The 2006 rulings did not create a monopoly. They removed a statutory permission for law graduates, leaving the general freedom of economic activity in place. The Ombudsman confirmed the gap in 2023, noting "brak systemowego rozwiązania w zakresie zasad świadczenia pomocy prawnej w Polsce" (no systemic rules on the provision of legal assistance in Poland). https://bip.brpo.gov.pl/sites/default/files/2023-08/Do_Senatu_pomoc_prawna_ustawa_opinia_27.07.2023.pdf (B)
- **Pending pressure for a monopoly.** On 14 Sep 2022 the Naczelna Rada Adwokacka petitioned the Senate for a law under which defined legal services "będą mogły być świadczone za wynagrodzeniem jedynie przez przedstawicieli zawodów prawniczych" (could be provided for payment only by members of the legal professions). In July 2023 the Ombudsman reported that "nie podjęto jeszcze nad nim prac legislacyjnych" (no legislative work had started). Same source (B). I found no later bill (D for the absence).
- **Correction to the brief.** The task described an opening of the "doradztwo prawne" market by "2006-2013 reforms". The primary sources show the market was never reserved by statute. The 2006 rulings struck a permission, not a ban. I did not verify what the 2013 deregulation changed (U), but it concerned access to the professions, not a reservation of advice.

### 2.2 Restrictions on software or AI giving company-specific assessments

- **No statute restricts it.** Neither professional act nor the AI law (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1003) regulates automated legal assessments by non-lawyers. I searched the AI law for "porad", "pomoc prawn" and "adwokat"; the only hits concern privileged communications during inspections and representation of the AI authority. (A)
- **Unfair competition and consumer law** still apply to misleading claims. In 2021 the competition authority UOKiK fined a law firm for advertising "free" legal analyses that in fact required a paid subscription (decision RLU-3/2021 of 21 Dec 2021, https://decyzje.uokik.gov.pl/bp/dec_prez.nsf/0/26AC66EE1951C34EC12587D700456B54/$file/Decyzja%20ZIK%20nr%20RLU-3-2021%20z%2021.12.2021%20r.-%20Kancelaria%20Prawna%20Legatis%20sp.%20z%20o.o.%20z%20siedziba%20w%20Warszawie.pdf, D because seen only in search results).
- **Court decisions 2021-2026 on legal tech or AI advice.** I found none (D for the absence).
- **Bar statements on AI.** The advocates' code now has § 23e, added by resolution 41/2026 of 12 Jun 2026: "Korzystanie z narzędzi technologicznych przez adwokata powinno mieć wyłącznie charakter wspomagający..." and "nie zwalnia adwokata z osobistej odpowiedzialności". Gloss: AI use by an advocate must be only supportive and does not relieve personal responsibility. It binds the partner lawyer, not Regulift. https://www.adwokatura.pl/admin/wgrane_pliki/file-zal-do-uchwaly-prezydium-nra-nr-1742026kodeksetykiadwokackiejtekst-jednolity-44572.pdf (A as the bar's own text). KIRP published AI recommendations for radcowie in force since 28 May 2025 (D, search snippet).

### 2.3 May a non-lawyer platform receive money from law firms?

- **Advocates, § 23d(2) Kodeks Etyki Adwokackiej (2026 text):** "Adwokatowi nie wolno przyjmować od innego adwokata lub osoby trzeciej korzyści tytułem wynagrodzenia za skierowanie klienta do innego adwokata, ani przekazywać komukolwiek korzyści tytułem wynagrodzenia za skierowanie doń klienta." Gloss: an advocate may not accept a benefit for referring a client, nor give anyone a benefit as payment for referring a client to him. Same URL (A).
- **§ 23d(1):** "Adwokata obowiązuje zakaz współpracy z podmiotami pozyskującymi klientów z naruszeniem prawa, zasad współżycia społecznego lub w sposób sprzeczny z godnością zawodu." Gloss: no cooperation with client-acquirers acting unlawfully or against the dignity of the profession. (A)
- **§ 23a(4):** "Adwokat nie może składać bezpośrednich propozycji świadczenia pomocy prawnej za pośrednictwem osób trzecich, które otrzymują za to wynagrodzenie." Gloss: no direct offers of legal services through paid third parties. (A)
- **Radcowie, Kodeks Etyki Radcy Prawnego art. 33(3) (text published Feb 2024):** "Radca prawny nie może pozyskiwać klientów za pośrednictwem lub przy pomocy osób lub podmiotów zewnętrznych dzieląc się z nimi wynagrodzeniem pochodzącym od pozyskanego w ten sposób klienta, jeżeli nie uczestniczą one w świadczeniu pomocy prawnej na rzecz tego klienta." Gloss: a radca may not acquire clients through outside entities by sharing with them the fee from the client so acquired, unless they take part in providing the legal assistance. https://kirp.pl/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/kodeks-etyki-radcy-prawnego-i-regulamin-wykonywania-zawodu.pdf (A)
- **Art. 34(1):** "Radca prawny nie może przyjmować wynagrodzenia lub innego świadczenia za skierowanie klienta do innego podmiotu świadczącego pomoc prawną lub usługi z nią związane." Gloss: a radca may not accept payment for referring a client. (A) A press report says a proposal to allow paid intermediation was rejected at the radcowie's national congress (rp.pl, C, year not confirmed).
- **Independence and advertising.** Advocates' § 7: "W czasie wykonywania czynności zawodowych adwokat korzysta z pełnej swobody i niezależności." § 23(3)(f) bans advertising that creates "choćby prawdopodobieństwo wywołania nieuzasadnionego oczekiwania co do wyników" and § 23a(2) makes the advocate responsible for advertising "opublikowaną przez osoby trzecie w jego imieniu lub na jego rzecz". Radca art. 32(1) bans advertising that is "niezgodne z rzeczywistością lub wprowadzające w błąd". (A)
- **Conclusion.** A flat workspace fee for concrete software services is not a referral benefit and is not a share of client fees, so it fits both codes. A fee per mandate or per referral falls under § 23d(2) and art. 33(3). A percentage of fees is plainly banned.

### 2.4 May non-lawyers own shares in law firms?

No, with one open question.

- **Prawo o adwokaturze art. 4a(1)-(2):** advocates practise in a sole office, a team, or a civil, general, partnership, limited or limited-joint-stock partnership in which the partners (for limited forms, the general partners, "komplementariuszami") are "adwokaci, radcowie prawni, rzecznicy patentowi, doradcy podatkowi lub prawnicy zagraniczni". "Wyłącznym przedmiotem działalności spółek ... jest świadczenie pomocy prawnej." Gloss: the firm's sole object must be legal assistance. (A)
- **Ustawa o radcach prawnych art. 8(1)-(2)** has the same list and the same sole-object rule. (A)
- **Open question.** For limited partnerships the statute names only the general partners. Whether a non-lawyer may be a limited partner ("komandytariusz") is not answered by the text. I did not find a ruling (U). Do not plan on it.

### 2.5 Developments 2025-2026

- **AI Act law.** Ustawa z dnia 3 lipca 2026 r. o systemach sztucznej inteligencji, Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1003, in force 11 Aug 2026. Art. 5(1): "Komisja Rozwoju i Bezpieczeństwa Sztucznej Inteligencji ... jest organem nadzoru rynku". Art. 91 lets the Commission run a regulatory sandbox for 6 to 12 months. https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/1003/text.pdf (A) Nothing in it targets legal services.
- **Advocates' act amendment.** Ustawa z dnia 4 września 2026 r., Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1290, in force 17 Oct 2026. It rewrites art. 4(1) to add "sporządzaniu dokumentów prawnych i ich projektów, w tym umów, regulaminów i statutów", still as a description, not a reservation. It adds criminal art. 95o: whoever without authority uses the title "adwokat" or labels a business "kancelaria adwokacka" or "kancelaria adwokatów" "podlega grzywnie od 5000 do 200 000 złotych". It also adds art. 95p: "Kto, nie będąc do tego uprawnionym gromadzi i udostępnia publicznie wszystkie lub niektóre informacje, o których mowa w art. 49 ust. 2 i 3, bez zgody okręgowej rady adwokackiej lub Naczelnej Rady Adwokackiej, lub prowadzi rejestr lub inne zestawienia obejmujące wszystkie lub niektóre dane wskazane w art. 49 ust. 2 i 3" faces the same fine. Art. 49(2)-(3) covers name, list number, contact details and up to five practice areas. Gloss: compiling and publishing advocates' list data, or running a register of it, without bar consent is a crime. https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/1290/text.pdf (A)
- **Radcowie act amendment.** Ustawa z dnia 15 maja 2026 r., Dz.U. 2026 poz. 731, in force 18 Jun 2026, new art. 8a: "Oznaczeniem „kancelaria radcy prawnego" lub „kancelaria radców prawnych" może posługiwać się wyłącznie kancelaria radcy prawnego lub spółka, o której mowa w art. 8 ust. 1." https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/731/text.pdf (A)
- **Impact on Regulift.** A public "partner lawyers" page listing Polish advocates with their practice areas may fall within art. 95p if read literally. Collect such data only from partner firms under contract, keep it inside the product, and ask the Naczelna Rada Adwokacka or a Polish lawyer whether partner consent is enough.

## 3. Belgium

### 3.1 Is paid out-of-court legal advice reserved to lawyers?

No. Only pleading and the title are reserved.

- **Code judiciaire art. 440:** "Devant toutes les juridictions, sauf les exceptions prévues par la loi, seuls les avocats ont le droit de plaider." Gloss: before all courts, save legal exceptions, only lawyers may plead. Justel, Code judiciaire, Deuxième partie (numac 1967101053), https://www.ejustice.just.fgov.be/cgi_loi/article.pl?language=fr&lg_txt=f&type=&sort=&numac_search=1967101053&cn_search=1967101052&caller=list&view_numac=1967101052f (A)
- **Art. 428:** "Nul ne peut porter le titre d'avocat ni en exercer la profession" without a Belgian law degree, the oath and enrolment. Gloss: the title and the profession of avocat are protected, but the Code does not define consultation as part of a reserved field. Same source (A).
- **The bar's own reading.** The Brussels French-speaking bar's council, minutes of 20 May 2014, quoting its 2010 rulebook: "Seul le titre d'avocat est protégé, ainsi que les activités dont l'avocat a le monopole. Les autres activités de prestations de services juridiques obéissent au droit commun de l'exercice ... d'une activité indépendante ou salariée, non protégée." Gloss: other legal services are an ordinary, unprotected activity. https://barreaubruxelles.be/assets/documents/Extrait-du-Proc%C3%A8s-verbal-de-la-s%C3%A9ance-du-20-mai-2014.docx (B)
- **The "2017 Loi on legal advice".** I found no Belgian statute of 2017 or later that reserves "consultation juridique" (U). Most likely the brief confused this with the French Loi du 31 décembre 1971, art. 54, which does reserve consultation in France. A Belgian lawyer should confirm this point.

### 3.2 Restrictions on software or AI giving company-specific assessments

- **No statute restricts it** (U for completeness, since I did not search the whole Code de droit économique). Consumer and market-practice rules on misleading claims apply. I did not verify article numbers (U).
- **The bars' joint AI guidelines (OVB and OBFG, early 2025).** The OVB page links them as "OVB OBFG Richtlijnen AI Advocaten". Quotes: "Het gebruik van AI is niet verboden, noch verplicht." "De advocaat is niet verplicht aan de cliënt mee te delen dat beroep wordt gedaan op AI". Confidential material may go into an AI tool only if the lawyer is sure it runs "in een gesloten omgeving met voldoende waarborgen". The PDF version adds: "De advocaat blijft steeds eindverantwoordelijke voor de output van de AI." Gloss: AI is neither banned nor required, clients need not be told, secrets stay in a closed environment, and the lawyer stays responsible. https://www.ordevanvlaamsebalies.be/nl/kennisbank/deontologie/gebruik-artifici%C3%ABle-intelligentie (A as the bars' own text). For Regulift this means partner lawyers may use the platform's AI features, but client secrets must stay in a closed, safeguarded environment. The hosting and data-sharing design has to support that.
- **Courts on AI misuse.** An OVB news item of 7 Apr 2026 reports a Raad van State judgment (no. 265.712) and a Hof van beroep Antwerpen judgment of 25 Mar 2026 (2025/AR/774) on litigants' misuse of AI. I did not open either judgment (B, relayed by the team lead's helper).
- **Court decisions 2021-2026 on legal tech or AI advice.** I found none (D for the absence). A 2017 practitioner article notes that a strict reading of the bar rules "leidt tot de vaststelling dat het zeer moeilijk is voor een advocaat om zijn diensten aan te bieden via zulke platformen". https://www.lexgo.be/en/news-and-articles/4341-zijn-legal-online-platformen-legaal-voor-de-advocaat (C)

### 3.3 May a non-lawyer platform receive money from law firms?

**Flemish bars, Codex Deontologie voor Advocaten (OVB), update to Belgian Official Journal of 9 Dec 2025:** https://www.ccbe.eu/fileadmin/speciality_distribution/public/documents/National_Regulations/DEON_National_CoC/NL_2026_Code-of-Conduct_OVB.pdf (A as the bar's text, CCBE copy).

- Art. 111 § 2: "De advocaat mag, behalve bij een samenwerkingsverband tussen advocaten, aan niemand een honorarium, voorschot of enigerlei andere vergoeding betalen als tegenprestatie voor de introductie van een cliënt." Gloss: a lawyer may pay no one any fee in return for introducing a client. Art. 286 repeats this for cross-border work.
- Art. 262: "Het is de advocaat verboden zijn honorarium te delen met iemand die geen advocaat is, tenzij een associatie tussen de advocaat en deze andere persoon toegelaten is door de wet en de beroeps- en gedragsregels". Gloss: no fee sharing with non-lawyers.
- Art. 178ter.2, on multidisciplinary networks: "De advocaat en de niet-advocaat delen geen infrastructuur. Zij delen evenmin computernetwerken en data waardoor de niet-advocaat toegang zou kunnen krijgen tot dossiers van de advocaat." Gloss: in a network, lawyer and non-lawyer share no infrastructure, computer networks or data that would give the non-lawyer access to the lawyer's files. A SaaS vendor is not a network member, but a Flemish bar could read a recommend-each-other arrangement as one. The shared workspace should keep the lawyer's matter files out of Regulift staff's reach.
- Advertising: art. 83 "De advocaat mag geen misleidende publiciteit voeren"; art. 84 § 2 bans an unsolicited "gepersonaliseerd dienstenaanbod voor een bepaalde zaak". (A)

**French- and German-speaking bars, AVOCATS.BE Code de déontologie de l'avocat, version in force 30 Apr 2026:** https://avocats.be/sites/default/files/2026-09/30.04.2026-code-deontologie-version-francaise-en-vigueur-au-30.04.2026-4.pdf (A as the bar's text).

- Art. 4.12 § 2, amended 15 Apr 2024, on online services: "Il ne rétrocède pas d'honoraires à un tiers pour ce type de prestation, sans préjudice de la possibilité de rétribuer ce tiers pour le service qu'il offre et les frais qu'il expose à cette occasion." Gloss: no fee rebate to a third party for online services, but the lawyer may pay that third party for the service it provides and the costs it incurs. This is the clearest rule for Regulift in any of the three countries, and it allows a flat workspace fee. The 2015 version was stricter: only "une participation dans les frais de gestion", "pour autant qu'elle ne soit pas liée à la nature de l'intervention de l'avocat".
- Art. 4.13: "L'avocat ne délivre aucun service ni ne donne de consultation ou d'avis personnalisé sur un forum de discussion électronique ou tout autre groupe virtuel public." Gloss: no personalised advice in public online forums. Keep lawyer opinions inside private workspaces.
- Art. 4.39 § 2: "L'avocat s'interdit de partager ses honoraires, d'en ristourner une partie au professionnel non-avocat avec lequel il coopère et d'en percevoir de celui-ci de quelque manière que ce soit. Toute forme d'honoraires ou de rétribution dite de présentation lui est également interdite. Toute obligation de réciprocité ou d'exclusivité souscrite envers un professionnel non-avocat est interdite." Gloss: no fee sharing, no presentation fee, and no reciprocity or exclusivity commitment towards a non-lawyer. So a partner firm may not promise Regulift exclusivity.
- Art. 5.3: personal advertising "n'est pas trompeuse ni dénigrante et ne contient pas de mentions comparatives". Art. 5.5 bars stating "un pourcentage de réussite". This limits how partner firms may be presented on Regulift.

### 3.4 May non-lawyers own shares in law firms?

No in Flanders. Effectively no in Wallonia and Brussels.

- OVB art. 178.1: "Een advocaat mag in het Vlaams rechtsgebied geen multidisciplinaire associatie vormen." Art. 178.2 requires the ban to be reviewed "minstens om de twee jaar". Art. 171.4 lists only lawyers as partners in lawyer partnerships. (A)
- OVB art. 171.5 is an exception: a Flemish lawyer may join a partnership "die buiten het Vlaams rechtsgebied werden opgericht en waarvan de aandelen mede door niet-advocaten worden aangehouden". Gloss: a firm set up outside Flanders with non-lawyer shareholders may be joined. This helps foreign alternative business structures, not a Belgian investment by Regulift. (A)
- AVOCATS.BE art. 4.43 § 1 (2026): only listed natural persons "peuvent participer au capital d'une société d'avocats": former partner-lawyers, spouses or partners, ascendants, descendants, heirs, and administrative staff. Under § 2, 2°, together they may hold neither "la moitié ou plus du capital" nor half the votes. Under § 2, 4°, they may not sit on the management body. A company such as Regulift is not on the list. (A)
- AVOCATS.BE art. 4.40 allows a "société de moyens" (cost-sharing company) only with members of a "profession agréée". Art. 4.41 lists these: accountants, tax advisers, doctors, notaries, bailiffs, auditors, architects, vets and pharmacists. A software company is not among them. (A)

- **NIS2.** Loi du 26 avril 2024 établissant un cadre pour la cybersécurité des réseaux et des systèmes d'information d'intérêt général pour la sécurité publique, numac 2024202344, published in the Moniteur belge on 17 May 2024. ## 4. Switzerland

### 4.1 Is paid out-of-court legal advice reserved to lawyers?

No. Only court representation is reserved, and only by cantonal law.

- **BGFA art. 2(1)**, SR 935.61, version of 1 Jul 2025: "Dieses Gesetz gilt für Personen, die über ein Anwaltspatent verfügen und in der Schweiz im Rahmen des Anwaltsmonopols Parteien vor Gerichtsbehörden vertreten." Gloss: the federal law applies to patented lawyers who represent parties before courts within the monopoly. https://fedlex.data.admin.ch/filestore/fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/cc/2002/153/20250701/de/html/fedlex-data-admin-ch-eli-cc-2002-153-20250701-de-html-1.html (A)
- **Federal Council report to Parliament**, 11 Apr 2018, on writing off motion 12.3372 (BBl 2018): advisory work "ist ... auf Stufe Bund nicht geregelt und kann somit von jeder Person ausgeübt werden, die dies beabsichtigt, ohne dass eine besondere Aufsicht oder Ausbildung erforderlich wäre. Die Rechtsberatungstätigkeit ist in der Schweiz nicht reguliert." Gloss: anyone may give legal advice without supervision or training; legal advice is not regulated in Switzerland. Footnote 4 adds that several cantons apply the BGFA to people who use the lawyer title for advisory work. https://www.newsd.admin.ch/newsd/message/attachments/51942.pdf (A)
- **Zurich, Anwaltsgesetz LS 215.1, § 11(1)**, current version 071: reserved to registered lawyers are "a. die Verteidigung und die berufsmässige Vertretung der Privatklägerschaft oder anderer Verfahrensbeteiligter im Strafprozess vor den Strafbehörden, b. die berufsmässige Vertretung im Zivilprozess vor den Schlichtungsbehörden und den Gerichten." § 40(1) fines a breach with "Busse bis 20 000 Franken". Gloss: only criminal defence and representation in civil proceedings are reserved. https://www.notes.zh.ch/appl/zhlex_r.nsf/WebView/562DA3E5A0EEF989C12577E10047DF30/$File/215.1_17.11.03_71.pdf (A)

### 4.2 Restrictions on software or AI giving company-specific assessments

- **No statute restricts it.** Switzerland has "aktuell keine spezifische Gesetzgebung zur KI" (Federal Office of Justice, https://www.bj.admin.ch/de/kuenstliche-intelligenz, A).
- **Unfair competition.** UWG art. 3(1)(b) bans "unrichtige oder irreführende Angaben" about one's own services. Art. 3(1)(c) bans "unzutreffende Titel oder Berufsbezeichnungen ..., die geeignet sind, den Anschein besonderer Auszeichnungen oder Fähigkeiten zu erwecken". SR 241, version of 1 Jan 2025, https://fedlex.data.admin.ch/filestore/fedlex.data.admin.ch/eli/cc/1988/223_223_223/20250101/de/html/fedlex-data-admin-ch-eli-cc-1988-223_223_223-20250101-de-html-5.html (A) Regulift must not call its output "Rechtsberatung durch Anwälte" unless a lawyer gives it.
- **Court decisions 2021-2026 on legal tech or AI advice.** None found (D for the absence). The SAV site lists an article titled "Die Wegleitung für den Umgang mit künstlicher Intelligenz des SAV", so the bar has AI guidance, but I did not open it (D).

### 4.3 May a non-lawyer platform receive money from law firms?

**SAV Schweizerische Standesregeln, version 1 Jul 2023:** https://www.sav-fsa.ch/documents/672183/3599428/Schweizerische_Standesregeln_1.7.23.pdf/9077385e-07f5-6236-9202-0df617750961?t=1694616000939 (A as the bar's text. These rules bind SAV members; cantonal supervisors use them to interpret the BGFA.)

- Art. 18: "Anwältinnen und Anwälte bezahlen Dritten keine Provisionen für die Vermittlung von Mandaten. Sie nehmen auch keine Provisionen an, wenn sie Dritten Mandate vermitteln." Gloss: no commissions for brokering mandates, either way.
- Art. 36: "Anwältinnen und Anwälte können ihre Anwaltsdienstleistungen über digitale Plattformen, wie Anwaltsverzeichnisse, Vermittlungs- oder Beratungsplattformen anbieten oder erbringen. Sie sorgen dafür, dass dabei insbesondere die Grundsätze der unabhängigen Berufsausübung (Artikel 3), der Wahrung des Berufsgeheimnisses (Artikel 4), des Verbots von Vermittlungsprovisionen (Artikel 18) und der Werbung (Artikel 25) eingehalten sind." Gloss: platforms are allowed if independence, secrecy, the commission ban and the advertising rules are respected.
- Art. 3: independence requires that lawyers "sich ... keinen Einflüssen von Dritten aussetzen, die ihrerseits nicht der berufsrechtlichen Aufsicht unterstehen".
- Art. 25: lawyers "dürfen nicht an Werbung für sich durch Dritte mitwirken", unless that advertising is truthful and factual.

**BGFA art. 12** (A): "b. Sie üben ihren Beruf unabhängig, in eigenem Namen und auf eigene Verantwortung aus." "d. Sie können Werbung machen, solange diese objektiv bleibt und solange sie dem Informationsbedürfnis der Öffentlichkeit entspricht." "e." bans an agreement "über die Beteiligung am Prozessgewinn als Ersatz für das Honorar".

Conclusion: a flat platform fee is allowed; a fee per mandate is a "Provision für die Vermittlung"; a share of fees breaches art. 18 and the independence rule.

### 4.4 May non-lawyers own shares in law firms?

Not in a firm whose lawyers are on a cantonal register. Yes in a firm that only advises.

- **BGFA art. 8(1)(d):** registered lawyers "müssen in der Lage sein, den Anwaltsberuf unabhängig auszuüben; sie können Angestellte nur von Personen sein, die ihrerseits in einem kantonalen Register eingetragen sind." (A)
- **Federal Supreme Court 2C_1054/2016 and 2C_1059/2016 of 15 Dec 2017 (BGE 144 II 147).** A Geneva branch of a firm with 39 partners, one of them a tax expert not on any register, was refused. The court reasoned that "le tiers non inscrit à un registre cantonal n'est soumis ni aux règles professionnelles, ni à la surveillance disciplinaire" (gloss: a non-registered third party is subject neither to the professional rules nor to discipline). It held that independence is assured only if "seuls des avocats inscrits puissent influencer la relation d'emploi". Full text via the DFR mirror, https://servat.unibe.ch/dfr/bger/2017/171215_2C_1054-2016.html (A; the mirror strips accents, which I restored). BGE 147 II 61 (2C_372/2020) confirmed the line (D, secondary).
- **The opening.** Because advice is unregulated, Regulift could own a Swiss subsidiary that employs patented but unregistered lawyers who give advice, but not court representation. They may not then work under the cantonal register rules. Footnote 4 of the 2018 report warns that some cantons extend the BGFA to such title users. Check the canton before doing this.

### 4.5 Developments 2025-2026 and Swiss AI regulation

- **AI regulation plan.** On 12 Feb 2025 the Federal Council told the justice department (EJPD) to prepare "bis Ende 2026 eine Vernehmlassungsvorlage für neue Regeln für die Nutzung von KI". The project covers "Transparenz, Datenschutz, Nichtdiskriminierung und Aufsicht". The page says that with these steps Switzerland "wird ... die KI-Konvention des Europarats ratifizieren können". The latest item on the page is the advisory group document of 17 Dec 2025. No consultation draft was published by 18 Oct 2026. https://www.bj.admin.ch/de/kuenstliche-intelligenz (A)
- **Council of Europe AI convention.** Switzerland signed it on 27 Mar 2025 (law.ch, C). It has not ratified it (A, from the page above).
- **European Convention on the protection of the profession of lawyer.** On 19 Jun 2026 the Federal Council asked the EJPD to analyse accession and report "bis spätestens Ende November 2027". https://www.bj.admin.ch/de/anwaltsrecht (A)
- **BGFA changes.** The version of 1 Jul 2025 adds UK lawyers to the free-movement rules. I found no revision on law-firm ownership (D for the absence).
- **Outside EU schemes.** Switzerland is outside NIS2 and the AI Act and not eligible for the EIT AI Lab, as research-countries.md states.

### 4.6 NIS2 and AI Act status

- **NIS2 equivalent.** Information Security Act revision, AS 2024 257, with reporting from 1 Apr 2025. Carried from research-countries.md (A there). Not re-checked in this session.
- **AI Act.** Not applicable. No national AI market-surveillance authority exists.
