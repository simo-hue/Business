# Legal-services rules in Denmark, Sweden and Finland for Regulift

Checked 2026-10-18. This replaces the "open (U)" label in research-countries.md section 4.6.

Grades: A is a primary statute, court or official rule text that we opened. B is an official secondary source, such as bar news or an authority press release. C is reputable press. D is a search snippet only. U is unverified.

Method. Three research passes, one per country, read the consolidated statutes and the current bar rule PDFs. Finlex renders with JavaScript, so the Finnish texts came from Finlex Open Data XML. advokatsamfundet.se has a broken certificate chain, so its PDFs were downloaded directly and converted to text. The quotes are verbatim from those files. This is research, not legal advice. Have a local lawyer in each country review the structure before launch.

## 1. Verdicts

**Denmark: partly open (high confidence).** Out-of-court legal advice to businesses is not reserved. Only the title "advokat" and court representation are reserved (retsplejeloven §§ 120, 131, 260). The only law on non-lawyer legal advice covers consumers, not businesses. So Regulift may tell a company directly that NIS2 applies to it or that it meets Article 32 GDPR. Marketing law still bans misleading claims about Regulift's status. The money and ownership side is closed, as in Austria. The bar rules ban fee sharing (art. 65) and any payment for referrals (art. 66). Law firm shares may be held only by lawyers and, below 10% in total, by the firm's own employees (§ 124 c). In February 2025 the Supreme Court also held that a law firm breaches good practice if a cooperation with an outside business puts its independence in reasonable doubt.

**Sweden: partly open (high confidence).** Anyone may give legal advice, and the bar says so itself. Only the title "advokat" is protected (rättegångsbalken 8 kap. 1 och 10 §§). In civil courts anyone the court finds suitable may act as representative (12 kap. 2 §). The money and ownership side is closed for advokatbyråer. VRGA 7.9.1 bans paying or receiving anything for bringing in mandates, fee splits are limited to other advokater, and only advokater may own an advokatbyrå (RB 8:4). Sweden has one opening the others do not use as much. Non-advokat "juristbyråer" are not bound by the bar rules. Since October 2025 a private-equity-backed group (AGRD, majority-owned by Axcel) has bought former advokatbyråer whose lawyers gave up the title.

**Finland: partly open (high confidence).** Out-of-court advice is not reserved. Only the titles "asianajaja" and "asianajotoimisto" (Advocates Act § 11) and general court representation (Code of Judicial Procedure 15:2) are reserved. The bar's Code of Conduct bans giving anyone "part of the fee or other compensation" for obtaining a mandate (TO 4.6). Only advocates working in the firm may own its shares (Advocates Act § 5(2) and the bar's licence conditions B 11). A Ministry of Justice working group is reviewing the structure of legal services, with a memo due by the end of 2026. Nothing published proposes reserving advice to lawyers.

**What changes from the earlier label.** "Open" is right for advice and wrong for money and ownership. In all three countries the partner firms face roughly the same bans on referral fees, fee shares and outside ownership as in Austria. The commercial model from section 5 of research-countries.md must stay in place for bar-member partner firms.

## 2. Denmark

### 2.1 What is reserved

Current consolidated text: retsplejeloven, LBK nr. 1298 af 07/11/2025, https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2025/1298/pdf (A). Sections 120, 124 to 124 g, 126, 131 and 260 are unchanged from LBK 1160/2024.

- **Title only, § 120 stk. 1.** "Personer, der ikke har fået beskikkelse som advokat … må ikke betegne sig som advokat eller benytte en anden betegnelse, der er egnet til forveksling hermed." People without a licence may not call themselves advokat or use a confusingly similar title. The penalty is a fine (stk. 2). (A)
- **Court cases, § 131.** "Advokater er eneberettigede til at udføre retssager for andre, jf. dog §§ 136, 260 og 730." Only advokater may conduct court cases for others. (A)
- **Appearing in court, § 260 stk. 2.** "Advokater er eneberettigede til at møde i retten som fuldmægtige for parterne, medmindre andet følger af stk. 3, 5, 6, 7, 9 og 10." Exceptions include a party's own employees and small claims. (A)
- **Consumer advice only, lov nr. 419 af 9. maj 2006 om juridisk rådgivning, § 1.** "Denne lov finder anvendelse på enhver, der i erhvervsmæssigt øjemed driver virksomhed med rådgivning af overvejende juridisk karakter, når modtageren af rådgivningen (forbrugeren) hovedsagelig handler uden for sit erhverv." The law applies only when the recipient acts mainly outside their trade. Adopted text: https://www.folketingstidende.dk/samling/20051/lovforslag/L65/20051_L65_som_vedtaget.pdf (A). It requires good practice, a written agreement, disclosure of insurance status and price, and no conflicts. The Consumer Ombudsman supervises it (§ 3).
- **Executive order BEK nr. 1720 af 15/12/2023**, https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2023/1720/pdf (A). Same consumer scope. § 3 stk. 4 bans any fee structure "som kan påvirke rådgiverens uafhængighed" (that may affect the adviser's independence).

Result: paid legal advice to businesses by non-lawyers is not regulated. The earlier note "§ 136" for title protection was wrong. Title protection is § 120.

### 2.2 Software and AI assessments

- No Danish statute restricts automated or AI legal assessments for businesses. No Danish court decision from 2021 to 2026 on this was found (U, absence not proven).
- **Marketing law applies business to business.** Markedsføringsloven, LBK nr. 1420 af 02/12/2024, https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2024/1420/pdf (A). § 3 stk. 1: "Erhvervsdrivende skal udvise god markedsføringsskik under hensyntagen til forbrugere, erhvervsdrivende og almene samfundsinteresser." Traders must show good marketing practice towards consumers, other traders and the public. § 20 stk. 1 bans practices "egnet til at vildlede … andre erhvervsdrivendes økonomiske adfærd" (likely to mislead other businesses). § 20 stk. 2 nr. 3 names "annoncørens status, egenskaber og rettigheder" (the advertiser's status and qualities). Retsinformation shows a version end date of 21 Apr 2026, so a later amendment may exist (U).
- **Sole traders.** If a customer is a sole trader acting outside their trade, the 2006 consumer law could apply. This boundary was not researched (U).
- **Bar statement on AI.** Advokaten, 4 Mar 2024, interview with the bar council chair, https://www.advokatsamfundet.dk/nyheder-medier/tidligere-artikler/2024/advokaten-1/2024-advokaten-1-tema-kunstig-intelligens-overvej-de-advokatetiske-faldgruber-nar-du-bruger-ai/ (B). "Regelsættet er så at sige teknologi-neutralt" (the rules are technology-neutral). "Man kan ikke som advokat fralægge sig det advokatetiske ansvar, fordi AI har været anvendt" (a lawyer cannot shed ethical responsibility because AI was used). No formal AI guidance from the Danish bar was found (U).
- **Confidentiality, AER art. 17 stk. 2.** "En advokat skal indhente klientens samtykke til deling af fortrolige oplysninger med eksterne personer." A lawyer needs the client's consent to share confidential information with outsiders (A). This governs data flowing from a partner firm into the platform.

### 2.3 Money from law firms

Source: De advokatetiske regler, in force 1 Jul 2025, https://advokatsamfundet.dk/media/qymm30zs/aer-2025-endelig-version.pdf (A). They bind lawyers, through "god advokatskik" in retsplejeloven § 126, not the platform.

- **Fee sharing, art. 65.** "En advokat må ikke indgå aftale om deling af sit honorar med nogen, der ikke er advokat." No fee-sharing agreements with non-lawyers. The only exception concerns the seller of a law practice.
- **Referrals, art. 66.** "En advokat må ikke kræve eller modtage honorar, provision eller andet vederlag for at henvise eller anbefale en klient. Tilsvarende må en advokat ikke betale honorar, provision eller andet vederlag for henvisning af en klient." No payment in either direction for referring a client.
- **Third-party commissions, art. 62.** "Provision, rabatter og lignende modtaget fra tredjemand … skal ubetinget godskrives klienten." Commissions from third parties go to the client.
- **Independence, art. 39 and art. 57.** "En advokat skal være uafhængig under udøvelse af sin virksomhed." Art. 57 bans fee terms "som kan påvirke advokatens uafhængighed … Dette gælder også ved honorering i form af kapitalandele" (including payment in shares).
- **Taking cases, art. 4 stk. 1.** A firm takes a case only "efter direkte anmodning fra klienten" (at the client's direct request), from another lawyer or from an authority. So the customer must instruct the firm itself.
- **Supreme Court, Højesteret, 5 Feb 2025, BS-35382/2024-HJR**, reproduced by the Disciplinary Board, https://www.advokatnaevnet.dk/advokatnaevnets-praksissamling/artikler/advokatfirmas-internationale-samarbejde-med-andre-virksomheder-var-i-strid-med-god-advokatskik/ (A; the domstol.dk PDF returned 403). Holding: a firm acts against good practice "hvis advokaten eller advokatselskabet indgår i et samarbejde med andre virksomheder på vilkår eller under omstændigheder, hvor der kan rejses rimeligt begrundet tvivl om, hvorvidt samarbejdet er foreneligt med kravene om iagttagelse af fortrolighed og bevarelse af uafhængighed og integritet." The network in that case set quality standards, required client names, and imposed exit penalties. No sanction was imposed because the question had been unsettled. It is settled now.

What follows for Regulift:

- **Flat workspace fee:** allowed in principle (our inference). The contract must not give Regulift control over quality standards, client choice or exit terms, and client data needs client consent.
- **Fee per mandate or referral:** not allowed (art. 66).
- **Share of fees:** not allowed (art. 65).

### 2.4 Ownership of law firms

- **§ 124 c stk. 1.** "Aktier eller anparter i et advokatselskab må … kun ejes af 1) advokater, der aktivt driver advokatvirksomhed i selskabet, dets moderselskab eller dets datterselskab, 2) andre ansatte i selskabet eller 3) et andet advokatselskab." Shares may be held only by practising lawyers in the firm, other employees of the firm, or another law firm. (A)
- **§ 124 c stk. 2.** Employees "må alene tilsammen eje under en tiendedel af aktierne eller anparterne i selskabet og må alene tilsammen have under en tiendedel af stemmerne". Non-lawyer employees together hold under 10% of capital and votes. The brief said "up to 10%". The text says "under 10%". (A)
- Employee owners must pass a partner exam (§ 124 d). Most of the board and management must be practising lawyers (§ 124 e). A law firm company may only practise law (§ 124 stk. 2).
- No passive outside capital. The bar's Nicolai Pii wrote in Advokaten, 4 Jun 2025, that the rule "indebærer et forbud mod passiv kapitalinvestering" (bans passive capital investment) (B). No reform was found (U).

Result: Regulift cannot hold a stake in a Danish law firm.

### 2.5 Developments 2025 to 2026

- **AI Act supplementary law in force.** LOV nr. 467 af 14/05/2025, "Lov om supplerende bestemmelser til forordningen om kunstig intelligens", https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2025/467/pdf (A). "§ 12. Loven træder i kraft den 2. august 2025." Nothing in it concerns legal services.
- **New AI bill pending.** 2026/1 LSF 3, "AI-loven", introduced 7 Oct 2026, https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/ft/202612L00003/pdf (A, bill text). It would replace lov 467 and let the minister designate market surveillance authorities by executive order. Digitaliseringsstyrelsen stays the single point of contact. The bill's remarks say the AI omnibus regulation postpones Annex III high-risk rules to 2 Dec 2027. That regulation itself was not opened (D for that detail). An earlier bill, 2025/1 LSF 111, appears to have lapsed (U).
- **Bar regulation.** Lov nr. 1464 af 10/12/2024 reorganised the bar's supervisory bodies. No change to fee, referral or ownership rules was found.

### 2.6 NIS2 and AI Act status

- **NIS2.** LOV nr. 434 af 06/05/2025, "NIS 2-loven", https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2025/434/pdf (A). "§ 33. Loven træder i kraft den 1. juli 2025." § 33 stk. 3: "Oplysningerne efter § 9, stk. 1, og § 10, stk. 1, skal indgives senest den 1. oktober 2025." Registration closed 1 Oct 2025. New entities register within three months (§ 9 stk. 2). Supervision is by sector under BEK nr. 620 af 02/06/2025, https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2025/620/pdf (A). Styrelsen for Samfundssikkerhed covers public bodies and several manufacturing sectors and runs registration on Virk.dk. Digitaliseringsstyrelsen covers cloud, data centres, managed service and security providers, and digital providers. Correction: the earlier table's dates were D. They are now A.
- **AI Act authorities.** Under lov 467 § 2 there are three competent authorities. Digitaliseringsstyrelsen is the notifying authority, single point of contact and market surveillance authority for most prohibited practices. Datatilsynet covers Article 5(1)(d) and (g). Domstolsstyrelsen covers courts. (A) Correction: the earlier table named only Digitaliseringsstyrelsen.

## 3. Sweden

### 3.1 What is reserved

Rättegångsbalken, consolidated up to SFS 2026:1657, https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/rattegangsbalk-1942740_sfs-1942-740/ (A).

- **Who is an advokat, 8 kap. 1 §.** "För riket skall finnas ett allmänt advokatsamfund. … Advokat är den som är ledamot av samfundet." An advokat is a member of the bar.
- **Title protection, 8 kap. 10 §.** "Den som utan att vara behörig till det utger sig för att vara auktoriserad som advokat i Sverige eller i en annan stat inom [EU/EES/Schweiz], döms till böter." Falsely claiming to be an advokat is fined.
- **Court representation, 12 kap. 2 §.** "Såsom ombud får inte brukas annan än den som rätten med hänsyn till redbarhet, insikter och tidigare verksamhet finner lämplig att vara ombud i målet." Anyone the court finds suitable may represent a party. There is no advokat monopoly in civil courts. The brief cited 12:22, which only covers court assistants.
- **Reserved roles.** Public criminal defenders must normally be advokater (21 kap. 5 §). Witness privilege covers "Advokater … och deras biträden" (36 kap. 5 §), not other advisers.
- **Employment, 8 kap. 2 §.** A person "anställd hos någon annan än advokat" cannot be admitted without an exemption. Regulift cannot employ practising advokater.
- **The bar's own statement**, https://www.advokatsamfundet.se/Anlita-en-advokat/ (B). "Det finns inte några krav på utbildning eller erfarenhet för att kalla sig jurist och erbjuda juridisk rådgivning i Sverige. Vem som helst kan starta en juridisk byrå och ge råd. Advokattiteln är däremot skyddad i lag." There are no requirements to give legal advice. Anyone may start a legal bureau. Only the advokat title is protected.
- **Backstop, lag (1985:354) om förbud mot juridiskt eller ekonomiskt biträde**, https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-1985354-om-forbud-mot-juridiskt-eller_sfs-1985-354/ (A). A court may ban an adviser who has grossly negligently assisted a crime. There is no licensing.

### 3.2 Software and AI assessments

- No Swedish statute restricts automated legal assessments. No court decision from 2021 to 2026 on AI or legal tech advice was found (U).
- **Marketing law applies business to business.** Marknadsföringslag (2008:486), https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/marknadsforingslag-2008486_sfs-2008-486/ (A). 1 § targets marketing "som är otillbörlig mot konsumenter och näringsidkare" (unfair to consumers and businesses). 10 § bans "felaktiga påståenden eller andra framställningar som är vilseledande", in particular about quality (pt 1) and "näringsidkarens egna eller andra näringsidkares kvalifikationer" (the trader's qualifications, pt 5).
- **Case example.** Realtid, 4 Oct 2024, https://www.realtid.se/juridik/advokatbyra-doms-for-vilseledande-marknadsforing/ (C). The Patent and Market Court held eleven claims by a law firm misleading, including "marknadsledande" (market-leading). The judgment itself was not opened.
- **Bar AI guidance.** "Vägledning om användning av generativ AI i advokatverksamhet", 12 Jun 2025, https://www.advokatsamfundet.se/globalassets/advokatsamfundet_sv/advokatyrket/vagledning-om-anvandning-av-generativ-ai-i-advokatverksamhet.pdf (A). "Advokatens ansvar för sin rådgivning gäller fullt ut oavsett om rådet helt eller delvis baseras på utdata från GenAI." The lawyer is fully responsible for advice built on AI output and must review it. It does not address AI vendors advising clients directly.
- **Bar guidance on external IT services**, updated April 2026, https://www.advokatsamfundet.se/globalassets/advokatsamfundet_sv/advokatyrket/uppdaterad-vagledning-om-anvandningen-av-externa-it-tjanster-i-advokatverksamhet-20260415.pdf (A). Partner firms must protect professional secrecy, so they will run vendor due diligence on Regulift.
- **Bar signals in 2026** (B). On 30 Sep 2026 the bar announced framework agreements with AI vendors for law firms, https://www.advokatsamfundet.se/Nyhetsarkiv/2026/september/nya-ramavtal-om-ai-tjanster-for-advokatbyraer/. Its business-law working group is examining "Advokatbyråers försäljning av AI-verktyg, legal tech och ägande i klientbolag" (law firms selling AI tools and legal tech), https://www.advokatsamfundet.se/Nyhetsarkiv/2026/juli/fokusfragor-for-arbetsgruppen-for-affarsjuridiska-fragor/.

### 3.3 Money from law firms

Source: Vägledande regler om god advokatsed med kommentarer, revised 5 Dec 2024, https://www.advokatsamfundet.se/globalassets/advokatsamfundet_sv/advokatetik/vagledande-regler-om-god-advokatsed-med-kommentarer-januari-2025.pdf (A). The same wording is in "Regler för advokatverksamhet 2025", https://www.advokatsamfundet.se/globalassets/advokatsamfundet_sv/advokatetik/regler-for-advokatverksamhet-2025.pdf (A).

- **Paying for mandates, VRGA 7.9.1.** "En advokat får inte avstå viss del av arvodet till annan eller lämna denne ersättning i annan form för att vederbörande tillfört eller tillför advokaten uppdrag eller utfäster sig att vara verksam i sådant syfte. Sådan ersättning får inte heller mottas." A lawyer may not give part of the fee, or any other payment, to someone for bringing in mandates or promising to. Receiving such payment is also banned.
- **Fee splits, VRGA 7.9.2 with 7.7.1.** Splits are allowed only with partners, associates or cooperating advokater. The 7.7.1 commentary says cooperation "innefattar inte en rätt att bedriva verksamhet med jurister som inte är advokater" (does not include practising with non-advokat lawyers).
- **Marketing by others, VRGA 7.8.3.** "En advokat får inte medverka till eller godta att annan bedriver ackvisitionsverksamhet eller marknadsföring för advokatens räkning på ett sätt som inte är tillåtet för advokaten själv." A lawyer may not let a third party market them in a way the lawyer may not.
- **Board statement of 1 Feb 2001 on cooperations**, https://www.advokatsamfundet.se/globalassets/advokatsamfundet_sv/advokatyrket/vagledande-uttalanden-m.m.pdf (A). "Vid lösliga samarbeten är det inte tillåtet att t.ex. lämna samarbetspartnern information om byråns klienter eller anvisningsprovision." In loose cooperations a firm may not give the partner client information or a referral commission.
- **Independence, VRGA 1 and 7.1.** "Advokaten skall som en oberoende rådgivare företräda och tillvarata klientens intressen." "En advokat skall bedriva sin verksamhet på ett sådant sätt att advokatens oberoende inte äventyras."
- The brief cited VRGA 4.6. That rule is about final invoicing. The relevant rule is 7.9.

What follows for Regulift:

- **Flat workspace fee:** probably allowed if it is not tied to mandates (our inference, no decision found, U). It resembles the AI-tool deals the bar promotes itself.
- **Fee per mandate or referral:** not allowed for advokat firms (7.9.1).
- **Share of fees:** not allowed for advokat firms (7.9.1, 7.9.2).
- **Juristbyråer:** non-advokat law firms are not bound by VRGA. With them only general law limits fee models.

### 3.4 Ownership of law firms

- **RB 8 kap. 4 § 2 st.** "I advokatverksamhet som bedrivs i bolagsform får endast advokat vara delägare eller bolagsman, om inte advokatsamfundets styrelse medger undantag." Only advokater may be owners unless the bar board grants an exception. (A)
- **Bar statutes § 38**, last amended 2 Jun 2022, https://www.advokatsamfundet.se/globalassets/advokatsamfundet_sv/advokatetik/stadgar-for-sveriges-advokatsamfund.pdf (A). Same rule.
- **VRGA 7.5.1 and 7.5.3** (A). Only advokater may be board members or shareholders. The only codified exception is a non-advokat CEO, with dispensation, holding "mindre än tio procent av kapitalet och … mindre än tio procent av röstvärdet" (under 10% of capital and votes).
- **Private equity route.** Bar news, 18 Dec 2025, https://www.advokatsamfundet.se/Nyhetsarkiv/2025/december/advokatsamfundet-har-startat-arbetsgrupp-for-affarsjuridiska-fragor/ (B). "I oktober 2025 ansökte ca 130 advokater på sex advokatbyråer om utträde med anledning av att ett riskkapitalbolag gick in som extern ägare." About 130 lawyers at six firms left the bar when a private equity firm became owner. Advokaten nr 4 2026 reports AGRD Partners, majority-owned by Axcel, with eight former advokatbyråer and about 200 lawyers who gave up the title, https://www.advokaten.se/tidigare-nummer/2026/nr-4-2026-argang-92/agrd-utmanar-advokater-med-stod-av-riskkapitalet/ (B).
- No inquiry or bill to open ownership was found (U).

### 3.5 Developments 2025 to 2026

- **No general AI Act supplementary law yet.** SOU 2025:101 "Anpassningar till AI-förordningen" proposed one, https://www.regeringen.se/rattsliga-dokument/statens-offentliga-utredningar/2025/10/sou-2025101/ (B, full text not opened). The only AI laws found are lag (2026:806) and förordning (2026:815) on real-time facial recognition for law enforcement (A, listing).
- **Interim authorities by government assignment.** PTS, 16 Jun 2026, https://pts.se/nyheter-och-pressmeddelanden/uppdrag-ger-pts-centralt-ansvar-enligt-ai-forordningen/ (B). PTS is national competent authority, "marknadskontrollmyndighet med ansvar för vissa förbjudna AI-användningsområden samt vissa AI-system med hög risk", single point of contact and sandbox operator. The assignment runs "till och med 31 december 2026", and "regeringen förbereder kompletterande nationell lagstiftning". IMY covers AI in law enforcement and credit scoring, https://www.imy.se/nyheter/imy-blir-marknadskontrollmyndighet-for-ai-forordningen/ (B).
- **Legal aid reform.** A June 2026 draft bill on court counsel and costs concerns criminal and legal-aid work only (B). Not relevant to business advice.
- No plan to regulate juristbyråer was found (D, old snippets only).

### 3.6 NIS2 and AI Act status

- **NIS2.** Cybersäkerhetslag (2025:1506), https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/cybersakerhetslag-20251506_sfs-2025-1506/ (A). "Denna lag träder i kraft den 15 januari 2026." 2 kap. 2 §: "Verksamhetsutövare ska så snart det kan ske anmäla sig till den myndighet som regeringen bestämmer."
- **Correction: the contact point is now FRA, not MCF.** The original cybersäkerhetsförordning (2025:1507) made MCF the single point of contact (23 §). SFS 2026:623, in force 1 Jul 2026, changed this: "23 § Försvarets radioanstalt ska vara gemensam kontaktpunkt", https://svenskforfattningssamling.se/sites/default/files/sfs/2026-05/SFS2026-623.pdf (A). Since 1 Jul 2026, registration and incident reports go to FRA. PTS supervises digital infrastructure, digital providers and managed ICT services (7 §).
- **AI Act.** PTS by government assignment until 31 Dec 2026, not by law (B). The earlier table's "PTS, pending" is broadly right.

## 4. Finland

### 4.1 What is reserved

- **The bar has been renamed.** It is now Suomen Asianajajat. asianajajaliitto.fi redirects to asianajajat.fi.
- **Advocates Act, laki asianajajista 496/1958**, consolidated to 543/2025, https://opendata.finlex.fi/finlex/avoindata/v1/akn/fi/act/statute-consolidated/1958/496/fin@20250543 and https://www.finlex.fi/fi/laki/ajantasa/1958/19580496 (A).
  - § 1: "Asianajaja on se, joka on maan yleisen asianajajayhdistyksen jäsenenä merkitty asianajajaluetteloon." An advocate is a bar member on the register.
  - § 11, title protection: "Jos se, joka ei ole asianajaja, käyttää itsestään asianajajan ammattinimitystä tahi toimistostaan nimitystä asianajotoimisto taikka muutoin ilmoittaa ammattialansa siten, että hänen sen perusteella voidaan erheellisesti olettaa olevan yleisen asianajajayhdistyksen jäsen, rangaistakoon sakolla." A non-advocate who uses the title, calls an office "asianajotoimisto", or suggests bar membership is fined.
  - § 13: "Tämän lain säännöksillä ei rajoiteta oikeutta toimia oikeudenkäyntiasiamiehenä tai -avustajana." The Act does not restrict acting as court representative.
- **Court representation, oikeudenkäymiskaari 15 luku 2 §**, https://opendata.finlex.fi/finlex/avoindata/v1/akn/fi/act/statute-consolidated/1734/4-000/fin@20260687 (A). "Jollei tässä tai muussa laissa toisin säädetä, oikeudenkäyntiasiamiehenä tai -avustajana saa toimia asianajaja, julkinen oikeusavustaja tai luvan saaneista oikeudenkäyntiavustajista annetussa laissa (715/2011) tarkoitettu luvan saanut oikeudenkäyntiavustaja." Only advocates, public legal aid attorneys and licensed legal counsel may represent in court, with exceptions such as in-house lawyers.
- **Licensed Legal Counsel Act 715/2011**, https://www.finlex.fi/fi/laki/ajantasa/2011/20110715. Only the table of contents rendered (A for structure, U for the body text).
- **The bar's own statement**, https://asianajajat.fi/oikeudellinen-apu/miksi-valita-asianajaja/mita-eroa-on-asianajajalla-ja-juristilla/ (B). "Juristi/lakimies ei ole lailla suojattu nimike." "Lawyer" or "jurist" is not a protected title.

Result: out-of-court advice to businesses is open. The brief's section numbers were wrong. Title protection is § 11, not § 1 or § 8.

### 4.2 Software and AI assessments

- No Finnish statute restricts software or AI legal assessments. No court, Market Court or bar disciplinary decision on legal tech advice from 2021 to 2026 was found (U).
- **Unfair Business Practices Act 1061/1978**, https://www.finlex.fi/fi/laki/ajantasa/1978/19781061 (A). § 1: "Elinkeinotoiminnassa ei saa käyttää hyvän liiketavan vastaista tai muutoin toisen elinkeinonharjoittajan kannalta sopimatonta menettelyä." § 2: "Elinkeinotoiminnassa ei saa käyttää totuudenvastaista tai harhaanjohtavaa ilmaisua, joka koskee omaa tai toisen elinkeinotoimintaa." No conduct against good business practice and no untrue or misleading claims about one's own business.
- The Consumer Protection Act chapter 2 covers consumer marketing only. It was not opened (U).
- **Bar AI guidance.** "Tekoälyopas asianajajalle", Annex 5 of the information security guide B 05.2, updated 13 Nov 2025, in force 1 Dec 2025, https://asianajajat.fi/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/B-05.2-Tietoturvaopas-hallitus-23.11.2018-paiv.-12.12.2019-24.9.2021-14.12.2023-19.4.2024-ja-13.11.2025-voimassa-1.12.2025-lukien.pdf (A). Point 2: "Riippumatta siitä, perustuuko neuvonta kokonaan tai osittain tekoälytyökalun tuottamaan sisältöön, asianajaja vastaa henkilökohtaisesti asiakkaalle annettavasta neuvosta." The advocate is personally liable for AI-based advice and must meet AI Act transparency duties. Point 4 allows AI only if privileged data is not used, stored or shared in breach of secrecy. This binds partner advocates, not Regulift.

### 4.3 Money from law firms

Source: Hyvää asianajajatapaa koskevat ohjeet, 15 Jan 2009, last amended 26 Jan 2023, https://asianajajat.fi/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/B-01-Hyvaa-asianajajatapaa-koskevat-ohjeet-15.1.2009-viim.-muut.-valt.k.-26.1.2023.pdf (A).

- **Payment for mandates, TO 4.6.** "Asianajaja ei saa antaa eikä luvata kenellekään osaa palkkiostaan tai muuta hyvitystä tehtävän saamisesta tai hankkimisesta." An advocate may not give or promise anyone part of the fee or other compensation for receiving or obtaining a mandate.
- **Independence, TO 3.2.** "Asianajajan on tehtävää hoitaessaan oltava riippumaton sellaisista ulkopuolisista vaikutteista, jotka voivat haitata hänen kykyään täysipainoisesti valvoa asiakkaansa etua."
- **Marketing by others, TO 10.2.** "Asianajaja ei saa antaa ulkopuolisen markkinoida asianajajan palveluita tavalla, joka asianajajalta itseltään olisi kiellettyä." Marketing must be truthful, factual and dignified.
- **No side businesses, TO 11.4.** An advocate may not run other business in the firm, nor "erottaa asianajotoiminnastaan erilleen toimintaa, jonka pääasiallisena sisältönä on oikeudellisten palveluiden tarjoaminen" (split legal services off into a separate business).
- **Catch-all, TO 1.1.** "kaikkea, mitä tapaohjeissa ei ole nimenomaisesti kielletty, ei välttämättä voida pitää sallittuna." What is not expressly forbidden is not necessarily allowed.

What follows for Regulift:

- **Flat workspace fee:** probably allowed if not tied to mandates (our inference, U).
- **Fee per mandate or referral:** not allowed for advocate firms (TO 4.6).
- **Share of fees:** not allowed for advocate firms (TO 4.6).
- Non-bar legal firms are not bound by the Code.

### 4.4 Ownership of law firms

- **Advocates Act § 5(2)** (A). "Asianajajan tointa ei saa harjoittaa yhtiössä muun kuin asianajajan kanssa, ellei asianajajayhdistyksen hallitus erityisistä syistä anna siihen lupaa." Advocacy may not be practised in a company with non-advocates unless the bar board allows it for special reasons. A limited company needs board permission. The brief cited § 5 b. That section lists foreign EU lawyers.
- **B 11 licence conditions**, 4 Oct 2019, https://asianajajat.fi/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/B-11-Yleiset-lupaehdot-asianajajan-toimen-harjoittamiseksi-osakeyhtiossa-hallitus-4.10.2019.pdf (A). Only advocates employed by the firm may own shares. Point 8.6 blocks transfers outside the profession.
- The bar wants law firms recognised in the Act and personal liability removed. It does not propose outside ownership (B).

## 5. What Regulift may do here that it may not do in Austria

"Bar firm" means a firm of advokater or asianajajat. Section 5 of research-countries.md gives the Austrian position.

| Activity | Austria | Denmark | Sweden | Finland |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Show applicability directly ("NIS2 applies to you") | No. Legal information is reserved (RAO § 8) | Yes. B2B advice is not reserved | Yes. Free advice market | Yes. Out-of-court advice is not reserved |
| Case-specific explanation of a legal rule | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Gap ratings ("you comply with Art. 32 GDPR") | No. A lawyer must sign | Yes, as Regulift's own assessment. No claim of advokat status | Yes, same limit | Yes, same limit |
| Filled templates (policies, contracts) | No for legal drafting | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Fee per mandate or referral from a partner firm | No (OGH 4 Ob 77/23m) | No for bar firms (AER art. 66) | No for bar firms (VRGA 7.9.1). Possible with juristbyråer | No for bar firms (TO 4.6) |
| Share of the firm's fees | No | No for bar firms (art. 65) | No for bar firms (7.9.1, 7.9.2). Possible with juristbyråer | No for bar firms (TO 4.6) |
| Flat workspace fee from a firm | Yes, priced for concrete services | Yes, if Regulift has no control over the firm (Højesteret 2025) | Probably yes, if not tied to mandates (U) | Probably yes, if not tied to mandates (U) |
| Stake in a law firm | No (RAO § 21c) | No (§ 124 c) | No in an advokatbyrå (RB 8:4). Possible in a juristbyrå | No (§ 5(2), B 11) |
| Marketing words | Avoid "rechtssicher" and "legal check" | Never "advokat" or similar (§ 120). No misleading status claims (markedsføringsloven § 20) | Never "advokat" (RB 8:10). No misleading claims on qualifications (MFL 10 §) | Never "asianajaja" or "asianajotoimisto" (§ 11). No misleading claims (1061/1978 § 2) |

Practical consequences for the product:

- **Direct output is the real gain.** In all three countries Regulift may show applicability verdicts, gap ratings and filled templates as its own output, without a lawyer's signature. It should label them as Regulift's assessment, not a lawyer's advice. It must disclose that they carry no legal privilege or professional insurance.
- **The partner model does not loosen.** With bar firms, keep the Austrian model: the client contracts the firm directly, Regulift charges a flat fee, and there are no per-mandate fees, fee shares or stakes. In Denmark, keep the workspace contract free of quality standards, client-name reporting and exit penalties imposed on the firm.
- **Juristbyråer are a Swedish option with a cost.** They may pay referral fees and take outside equity. They give up the advokat title, privilege and bar supervision, which may matter to Regulift's customers.
- **Client consent for data.** Partner firms need client consent before sharing confidential data with Regulift (Danish art. 17 stk. 2, Finnish TO and AI guide point 4, Swedish IT guidance).
