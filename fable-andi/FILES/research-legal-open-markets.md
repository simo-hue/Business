# Open markets, verified: who may advise, who may pay whom

Written 18 October 2026. Consolidates three Opus research reports written the same day against
primary statutes, bar codes and court decisions: `research-legal-it-nl.md` (Italy, Netherlands),
`research-legal-nordics.md` (Denmark, Sweden, Finland) and `research-legal-pl-be-ch.md` (Poland,
Belgium, Switzerland). Grades as in `research-countries.md`: A primary text, B official secondary,
C press or survey, D snippet, U unverified. This is research, not legal advice.

## The one-line result

"Open" was right for advice and wrong for money. In all eight countries Regulift may tell a
company directly that NIS2 applies to it, explain its case, rate its gaps and fill templates,
which Austria forbids. In all eight the bar codes still ban fees per mandate, referral commissions
and fee shares, and almost all ban outside ownership of law firms. The Austrian partner model
(flat workspace fee for concrete services, client contracts the firm, no equity link) is therefore
the single EU model. Italy drops from "open" to "partly open".

## New facts since the earlier research

- Italy: Law 137/2026 (legal profession reform) in force 16 Aug 2026 keeps the art. 2(6) wording and
  orders a rule voiding payments to non-lawyers for litigation-connected advice; decree in first
  reading 2 Oct 2026. D.Lgs. 179/2026 (in force 23 Oct 2026) adds AI training to lawyers' continuing
  education. Art. 13 of Law 132/2025 binds the law firm, not the software vendor.
- Netherlands: the draft AI Act implementation law makes the data protection authority (AP) the
  default supervisor with AP and RDI coordinating; consulted to 1 Jun 2026, not passed. "RDI pending"
  in the earlier table is superseded.
- Denmark: NIS2 dates now grade A (in force 1 Jul 2025, registration closed 1 Oct 2025). AI Act
  authorities are Digitaliseringsstyrelsen, Datatilsynet and Domstolsstyrelsen (LOV 467/2025); a
  replacement bill LSF 3 was introduced 7 Oct 2026. Supreme Court 5 Feb 2025: a firm breaches good
  practice if a business partnership puts its independence in reasonable doubt (quality standards,
  client-name reporting, exit penalties imposed by the partner). The workspace contract must avoid
  all three.
- Sweden: NIS2 single point of contact moved from MCF to FRA on 1 Jul 2026 (SFS 2026:623). No AI Act
  law; PTS and others act under a government assignment to 31 Dec 2026. PE-backed AGRD/Axcel has
  bought ex-advokatbyråer since Oct 2025 whose lawyers left the bar.
- Finland: AI Act law 1377/2025 in force 1 Jan 2026, Traficom single point of contact, Data
  Protection Ombudsman for prohibited practices. Ministry of Justice review of legal-services
  structure, memo due end 2026.
- Poland: Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1290, in force 17 Oct 2026, criminalises unauthorised use of "kancelaria
  adwokacka" (art. 95o) and compiling or publishing advocates' list data without bar consent
  (art. 95p). A public partner-lawyer directory needs bar consent in Poland. NIS2 fines only from
  3 Apr 2028 (art. 35); "registration closed Oct 2026" is not supported.
- Belgium: NIS2 law numac 2024202344 in force 18 Oct 2024, now A (art. 98). Joint OVB and AVOCATS.BE AI
  guidelines of 31 Jan 2025 (A): AI neither banned nor required, no duty to tell the client, lawyer
  stays responsible, confidential material only in a closed environment with safeguards (relevant
  to how the shared workspace is hosted). Lawyers may not give personalised advice in public online
  forums (art. 4.13), so lawyer opinions stay in private workspaces. No AI Act authority on the
  Commission list of 7 Sep 2026.
- Switzerland: no AI law; the consultation draft due end 2026 was not published as of 18 Oct 2026.
  Council of Europe AI convention signed 27 Mar 2025, not ratified.

## What this changes in the product

1. The country setting has two states for advice (closed: Austria, Hungary, France, Luxembourg,
   Czechia; Germany strict with safe harbour) and one open state (all eight above), with Italy as
   "open for readiness, lawyer for anything touching disputes or sanctions".
2. The money model does not change by country. Flat workspace fee everywhere. The only country
   where a referral fee would be lawful is the Netherlands, and only disclosed and fixed. Do not
   build it.
3. Marketing: never a lawyer title in any language. Poland criminalises "kancelaria adwokacka" and
   "kancelaria radcy prawnego" for non-lawyers.
4. Partner directory: in Poland, publishing advocates' list data needs bar consent.
5. Denmark: the partner contract must not impose quality standards, client-name reporting or exit
   penalties on the firm.

## Not confirmed anywhere

No court or bar decision in any of the eight countries on software platforms or AI giving legal
assessments, 2021 to 2026. Full Cassazione texts (italgiure timed out), the Italian bar code site
(geo-blocked), the Polish NIS2 registration schedule, Romania.
